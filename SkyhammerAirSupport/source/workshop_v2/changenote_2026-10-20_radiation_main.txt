New: nuclear fallout.
- The tactical nuke now leaves a radioactive zone around ground zero. It fades over 72 game hours by default and shows on the sensor map. Walk in and a warning sign shows on screen and Aegis warns you on the radio.
- The longer you stay, the bigger your dose. You get sick through the game's own sick moodle, and at the worst stage you keep losing health even after you leave. Sandbox option "Fallout can kill" (on by default).
- Protection: hazmat suit 95%, gas mask or NBC mask with a filter 70%, improvised gas mask or builder's respirator 40%. Worn together, up to 98%. A roof halves it.
- New item: Prussian Blue capsules (pharmacies, hospitals, labs, military medical storage). For 8 hours your dose clears 3x faster outside the zone. It is not an instant cure.
- NPCs get exposed too: Bandits, A-Life people and Companion Dogs' companions. Zombies are not affected.
- Fallout can be turned off or tuned in the sandbox.
Fixed: the sandbox option "Strikes damage vehicles" showed its raw name. It now shows its name and description in English, Korean and Chinese.
