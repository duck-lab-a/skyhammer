- New: A-10 napalm, carried on the same sortie as its gun runs, with its own button. Four canisters set a 40-tile strip on fire for 30 seconds; the infected caught in it burn and fall. Mod Options: napalm runs per sortie (1, 0-4) and burn time.
- New: B-2 tactical nuke (B61). Pick it with the B-2's weapon button before the call and mark a point 60-300 tiles away. A 15-second siren countdown and the warhead falling, then the flash, the shockwave and a mushroom cloud: every infected within 140 tiles dies. The B-2 then rearms for 2 hours by default, set in Mod Options or, online, the server's sandbox (600 points in killstreak mode). Single player: Mod Options. Online it is off unless the server's sandbox allows it (hurting players is a separate option, off by default).

- Changed: the terminal's mark buttons carry the weapon's name, icon and colour; the B-2's weapon button shows its rearm time on hover.
- Changed: the AC-130's engine fades out as it leaves instead of stopping.
- Changed: radio calls for the nuke and the napalm use real brevity; an urgent call cuts in at once.
- New sounds: real recordings (BigSoundBank, CC0) for the siren, the nuke and the napalm.
