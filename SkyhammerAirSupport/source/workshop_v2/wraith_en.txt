[h1]Boots on the ground.[/h1]
An add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813483790]Skyhammer Air Support[/url]: a new terminal tab that calls [b]Wraith Six[/b], a five-man special operations team flown in by [b]Ferryman Three-Two[/b].

Works with either edition of Skyhammer: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813483790]Viewpoint[/url] or [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813973682]Vanilla[/url].

[b]Single player only.[/b] Made for Build 42.21.

[img]https://i.ibb.co/hFJz5wft/TEXT-ADDON-aegis-gasmask.png[/img]

[h1]How it works[/h1]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/N2JzTYMV/terminal-wraith.png[/img]
[olist]
[*]Open the Skyhammer terminal and pick the [b]WRAITH / TEAM[/b] tab.
[*]Press [b]CALL[/b]. A helicopter drops five operators at a landing zone near you.
[*]For [b]2 minutes[/b] they move with you in a [b]diamond formation[/b] (point, flanks, rear) and fight the infected. [b]REGROUP[/b] pulls them in right around you, [b]SPREAD OUT[/b] puts them back in the diamond.
[*]When time is up or you press [b]RECALL[/b], the helicopter flies them out.
[/olist]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/Nd0srDPF/wraith-01-team.jpg[/img]

[h1]Good to know[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]The infected cannot kill them.[/b] An operator [b]you[/b] shoot or hit is pulled out on the spot.
[*]Own black tactical suit with the Wraith Team shoulder patch, and their own gear set: helmet, quad NVG, headset, neoprene hood, gas mask with a comms unit (side filter, or a hose to a filter pack on the back), plate carrier; M16 with red dot and laser, pistol and knife. No other gear mod needed.
[*][b]Suppressed[/b] rifles and pistols: quiet shots that draw far fewer infected.
[*]Short tactical callouts instead of the Bandits voices and swearing.
[*]Skyhammer's aircraft never hit them; green on the sensor map.
[*]The team and its landing zone carry over a save and reload.
[/list]

[h1]Settings[/h1]
Arrival [b]60 s[/b], on the ground [b]2 min[/b], cooldown [b]60 min[/b]. Change them in Mod Options ("Skyhammer - Wraith Team"). Real time; stops while paused.
In Skyhammer's [b]killstreak mode[/b] the team costs [b]50 points[/b] instead of a cooldown, the cheapest support.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*]Skyhammer Air Support, either edition: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813483790]Viewpoint Edition[/url] (with ZombieBuddy and Viewpoint) or [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813973682]Vanilla Edition[/url]

[b]Note:[/b] Steam lists both Skyhammer editions as required items, but you only need [b]one[/b] of them. Subscribe to the one you play with and enable only that one; you can skip the other.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]The tab says it needs Bandits?[/b] Enable Bandits and restart.
[*][b]Existing save?[/b] Yes.
[*][b]More hostile bandits?[/b] No. The team only appears when you call it.
[*][b]Drove far away?[/b] They can't follow a car; anyone left behind is picked up when that area loads again.
[*][b]Multiplayer?[/b] No. The Vanilla Edition plays online, but this add-on stays off there.
[*][b]All black? That's not tactical.[/b] You're right, it isn't. Real teams wear camo that matches the ground. The Wraith Team wears black purely because it looks cool. That's the whole reason.
[/list]
