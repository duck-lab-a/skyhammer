[h1]The sky has your back. No other mod needed.[/h1]
The Vanilla Edition of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813483790]Skyhammer Air Support[/url]: call an [b]AH-64 Apache[/b], an [b]A-10 Warthog[/b], an [b]AC-130 gunship[/b] and a [b]B-2 bomber[/b] over the radio once you have earned them. The aircraft show as their [b]shadows sweeping the ground[/b].

[b]Single player and multiplayer (experimental).[/b] Made for Build 42.21.

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/hFfsR26y/vanilla-05-terminal.jpg[/img]
[olist]
[*]Turn on the [b]air support radio[/b] and keep it on you.
[*]Press [b]K[/b] or right-click the radio to open the terminal.
[*]Pick a tab and press [b]CALL[/b].
[*]For the 105mm, the A-10 and the B-2, mark the target on the sensor map or in the game view ([b]EYES-ON[/b]).
[/olist]

[h1]Air support[/h1]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/Q3HH5r28/SUPPORT-STRIP.png[/img]

[h2]Hellhound One-One - AH-64 Apache[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/p6h4fmgj/vanilla-01-apache.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*]Escorts you, firing 30mm at nearby infected.
[*]Rocket salvos into big groups, never near you.
[*][b]Illumination flares[/b]: light up a marked spot without drawing infected.
[/list]

[h2]Valkyrie Two-Six - A-10[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/xKxgFmjR/vanilla-02-a10.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]GAU-8 gun runs[/b]: mark one point and the pilot strafes a 40-tile line through it, in the direction that catches the most infected, never near you.
[*]Between runs, mark a new point or it flies the line back.
[*][b]Napalm[/b] on the same sortie: mark a point and a 40-tile strip goes up in flames.
[/list]

[h2]Banshee Two-One - AC-130[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/QF9r7gL1/vanilla-03-ac130.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]40mm[/b]: fires on its own at the infected around you, or where you aim it. Hold and resume fire from the terminal.
[*][b]105mm[/b]: drops shells where you mark.
[/list]

[h2]Titan Zero-One - B-2[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/TDgNpXL2/vanilla-04-b2-nuke.jpg[/img]
[list]
[*][b]Heavy bombs[/b] carpet the area you mark.
[*]The blasts draw infected from far away.
[*][b]Tactical nuke[/b] (pick it with the weapon button before calling): mark a spot 60-300 tiles away; 15 s later every infected within 140 tiles dies.
[/list]

[h2]Fallout[/h2]
[img]https://i.ibb.co/jPXGhQW1/fallout-small-en.png[/img]
[list]
[*]The nuke leaves fallout behind. Ground zero stays radioactive for days, and the longer you hang around the sicker you get. Stay too long and it can kill you even after you get out.
[*]Wear a hazmat suit or a gas mask with a filter. Prussian Blue capsules flush it out faster, but they won't save you if you stay in there.
[*]NPCs get sick too.
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shots and blasts only hit infected [b]outdoors[/b] (unless "Blasts reach indoors" is on). In single player the B-2's bombs go through roofs, up to three floors.
[*]Friendly [b]Bandits[/b] and [b]A-Life[/b] NPCs are never hit and show in green on the sensor map.
[*][b]Highly compatible[/b]: played with about 80 mods with no conflicts found.
[/list]

[h1]Killstreak mode[/h1]
[b]Points, not cooldowns[/b]: every infected you kill yourself is a point (air support kills earn none), and points call the aircraft (Apache 80, A-10 120, AC-130 150, B-2 300, nuke 600). Recall before arrival for a refund. Turn it on in the sandbox or Mod Options.
[img]https://i.ibb.co/n8jjJ5Z4/vanilla-05-killstreak-420.jpg[/img]

[h1]Earn your air support[/h1]
New games start with a short quest (sandbox [b]Air support unlock[/b]). The key is one [b]relay[/b] (one per server online): a satellite dish on a wall + a military ham radio within 6 squares + generator power. Right-click the dish, then call Aegis Control (air support HQ) on the [b]air support radio[/b] and all four aircraft are yours.
[img]https://i.ibb.co/Y7N1j60z/relay-420.jpg[/img]
Don't want the quest? Pick [b]Any two-way radio[/b] in the sandbox.

[h1]Multiplayer (experimental)[/h1]
[list]
[*]The server and every player need this mod.
[*]Each player calls their [b]own[/b] aircraft; the [b]nuke[/b] only if the server allows it.
[*]Cooldowns and ammo are each player's own settings (held at the defaults with PvP on).
[*][b]PvP[/b]: with the sandbox's [b]Air support PvP[/b] on, air support hurts other players too, and an Apache can be shot down. Your side and indoors stay safe, and players are warned. Off, others only see and hear the blasts.
[*][b]Fallout[/b] hurts everyone standing in it, PvP or not.
[/list]

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Existing save?[/b] Yes. A save that already used air support keeps working on any radio; the quest starts in new games.
[*][b]Terminal won't open?[/b] The radio must be on and have battery. With the quest on, only the air support radio opens it; K alone opens the quest log.
[*][b]No shadows?[/b] Outdoors only, with the shadows option on; at night only over lit ground.
[/list]
