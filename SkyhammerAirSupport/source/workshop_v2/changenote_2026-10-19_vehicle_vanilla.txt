- New: strikes damage vehicles. The AC-130's 105mm, the A-10's gun run, the B-2's bombs and the tactical nuke now damage nearby vehicles: the closest are wrecked (flat tyres, smashed glass, a dead engine, so they will not drive), farther ones get flat tyres, cracked glass and dents. Sandbox option "Strikes damage vehicles" (on by default). In multiplayer the server applies it, so every player sees the same damage.
- Changed: the 105mm's fireball and earth column linger a little longer; the first moment of the blast is the same.
- Optimized: blasts and napalm fire make less work every frame while they are on screen.
