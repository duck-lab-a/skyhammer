=== Main (3813483790)
- Changed: future Viewpoint updates no longer switch the 3D aircraft and cockpit scenes off by themselves. At start-up the mod now checks that the parts of Viewpoint it uses are still there, and only turns the 3D view off if one of them has changed. ZombieBuddy will ask once more to approve the updated Skyhammer Java.
