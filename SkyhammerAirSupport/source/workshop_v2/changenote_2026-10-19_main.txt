Explosion effects and audio update
- Reworked blast fireballs, dirt plumes, lingering smoke, debris and ground dust rings.
- Increased the early fireball size for a stronger initial blast.
- Updated 30mm, 40mm, rocket, 105mm and B-2 impact sounds using the new explosion sound set.
- Radio voices, damage and strike timing are unchanged.
- Strengthened 105mm impacts and the A-10 flyby.
- B-2 bombs are much louder, heard from much farther away, and roll on for seconds, so a bombing run rumbles past.
- Smoother big strikes: the infected a B-2 run or the tactical nuke takes down now fall over a few frames instead of all in one, to cut the half-second freezes (single player).

- 폭발 화염, 흙기둥, 잔류 연기, 파편과 지면 먼지 고리를 개선했습니다.
- 초반 불꽃 크기를 키워 폭발 순간의 시각적 효과를 강화했습니다.
- 30mm, 40mm, 로켓, 105mm, B-2 착탄음을 새 폭발음 세트로 변경했습니다.
- 무전 음성, 피해량과 폭격 타이밍은 그대로입니다.
- 105mm 착탄음과 A-10 통과음을 보강했습니다.
